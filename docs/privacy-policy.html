Trading Assistant Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Trading Assistant is a market research application for publicly available market data. This policy explains the information handled by the app.

Information you provide

The app does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, financial account credentials, or broker credentials. The primary input is a stock ticker symbol that you choose to analyze.

Information sent for analysis

When you analyze a ticker, the ticker symbol is sent to the Trading Assistant API so the service can retrieve market data and calculate technical indicators and an explainable research signal.

Third-party data services

The backend uses public market-data services, including yfinance, to obtain market information. Trading Assistant does not execute trades or connect to brokerage accounts.

Service operations

The API may process standard technical request information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service, such as timestamps and network information. We do not use this information to create a user account or profile.

Children

Trading Assistant is not directed to children and is intended for general market research use.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be published at this location.

Contact

For questions about this policy or the app, use the support/contact channel provided with the Trading Assistant App Store listing.
